About Me

I am Enxuan Ruan (阮恩暄), a senior undergraduate student in the Department of Automation, College of Electrical and Engineering, at Sichuan University.

My research interests focus on robot learning and humanoid whole-body control.

Research Vision

My vision is to enable robots to integrate naturally into people's everyday lives, helping make life more convenient and intelligent.
